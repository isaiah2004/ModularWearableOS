Strap layout: 1.43-B watch + add-ons

All units mm. Wrist modelled as an ellipse (60:42 aspect) with the circumference set at the top left; pods are packed outward from the watch automatically. Parts are placed by arc length s along the strap from the top of the wrist (+ = 12 o'clock side, − = 6 o'clock side). Checks below are computed from the geometry; part sizes marked ~ are typical/unverified and should be measured.
Pods, 12 o'clock side: navigation (GPS, compass, BME280) · swap pack A · cellular (A7672S modem + call audio, on the flatter underside). 6 o'clock side: interface (gesture, UV, motor, NFC) · audio · hub (nRF52840: LoRa, ECG, motion-capture receiver) · swap pack B. Under the watch: health core (PPG, skin temp, EDA and ECG electrodes). Packs feed one VBAT wire through ideal diodes, so either can be pulled while the other runs the watch. Untick GPS module to use the modem's built-in GNSS instead.
