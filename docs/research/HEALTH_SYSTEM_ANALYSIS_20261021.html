The immediate calorie problem

svc_activity calculates daily burn as steps × 0.04 kcal and distance as steps × 0.75 m. Energy explicitly shows activity calories only. Sports separately calculates gross calories as MET ×3.5 ×kg /200 ×minutes; it never feeds the daily Energy total. [source, Energy, Sports]

Total = estimated rest + estimated net activity

Build a shared ledger before adding new cards. Workout gross energy must lose its resting component, and overlapping step energy must be replaced. Otherwise the new total will count the same expenditure twice.

Space before features

Archived source-exact app is 7,769,440 B in a 7,864,320 B OTA slot: 94,880 B headroom. Internal free RAM was 37,507 B at one observed pose. Budget and reclaim space first; broader refactoring can expand effort. No silent repartition or NVS erasure.

Raw logs and battery

Source path can emit 50 × 64 × 86,400 = 276,480,000 bytes/day of IMU records if continuously enabled (276.48 MB, excluding overhead). This is a derived volume, not a measured drain cause. Keep causal diagnostics, but bound raw capture and retain compact aggregates normally.

Human sleep is not OS sleep

Ordinary display-off retains services/IMU; explicit deep sleep parks the board and wakes via GPIO0. Overnight health needs an explicit acquisition policy and visible gaps. Motionless is neither proven sleep nor proven nonwear.

Accuracy and time to qualify

Current static review proves data paths, not sensor accuracy, classifier accuracy or present battery life. Two badminton recordings cannot validate five sports or opposite wrist/hand combinations. Multi-night/long-session field work adds calendar time.

A setup process that is usable on a watch

Reuse the existing local Phone Setup page for the larger form, with a complete watch editor as fallback. No new native phone app, cloud account or health-sensor purchase is required for modeled resting calories.

  1. Open Health Setup on watch or its temporary local Phone Setup page. Existing setup page currently covers Wi-Fi/essentials, not health.
  2. Explain measured movement versus modeled expenditure; choose units and enter the minimum profile.
  3. Validate values and show missing fields; offer an explicit estimate-model choice or resting-energy override.
  4. Confirm wrist and per-sport hands; offer stride calibration and optional goals.
  5. Review the values and assumptions, save through checked SD banks, and show pending/retry/saved honestly.
  6. Land on one Health overview. Every field stays editable; later edits create effective-dated revisions rather than rewriting past workouts.

Required to personalize resting energy

  • Units and local time zone; clock trust shown separately
  • DOB or age with effective date and model eligibility
  • Height, with cm/in conversion
  • Decimal body mass, measurement date and source
  • Explicit equation coefficient choice where needed, or a custom/measured resting-energy override with provenance

Missing means unknown. Allow Skip and show what remains unavailable. Do not prefill 70 kg, 175 cm, a birth date or a physiological coefficient as facts. Adult-population equations must not silently be used outside their eligibility.

Activity and wearing context

  • Watch wrist: left/right/unknown
  • Dominant hand separately from per-sport playing hand
  • Cricket batting and bowling hand independently
  • Walking/running stride or measured calibration
  • Optional step/movement/workout/sleep/water goals and reminders

Keep current guided wrist calibration but require confirmation when uncertain. Both supplied badminton sessions are right-hand play with left-wrist watch; this does not establish general dominance or validate other hand/wrist combinations.

Optional records with a purpose

  • Dated weight history and later corrections
  • Sleep schedule, manual bedtime/wake/nap corrections
  • Editable food kcal and water mL entries; named items if useful
  • Breathe session duration and optional subjective notes
  • Optional future manual/import body measurements with source and units

Collect personal values only for implemented features. No need for diagnoses or medication lists merely to turn on calorie estimates. Sensor-absent HR/SpO₂ setup is not a blocker.

One authoritative profile, with history.

Keep units, timezone, wrist, hands and global goals in authoritative nos_prefs. Import known Sports mass once into the checked health profile, then route Sports weight edits through that owner. DOB/age, height, dated mass and model choices live in the versioned health schema. One combined editor exposes them without duplicate writable copies. Preserve dirty retry and effective-date history.

IMU / manual inputs → typed observations → durable health journal → daily/session summaries → shared snapshot → every app and face.

One logical typed profile with explicit field owners. nos_prefs remains authoritative for units, timezone, wrist, hands and existing global goals. The health profile owns DOB/age, height, dated decimal mass and energy-model choice/override, with known/unknown, source and revisions.

Separate paired /watch/health/profile.{0,1}.bin with existing SD lease/lock, CRC, fsync/readback and dirty retry. One-time durable import of known Sports mass; then Sports reads/writes mass through the profile adapter. Preserve old migration source until commit succeeds; never keep two writable mass authorities. Calorie accounting contract

  • Resting energy is a model estimate, not a sensor reading. Use a supported resting-energy equation or explicit override only after profile validation.
  • Daily total expenditure = modeled resting energy + net active expenditure. Intake is a separate ledger; food minus total expenditure can be shown only with coverage/estimate limitations.
  • Sports currently reports gross MET expenditure. Convert consistently to net active before adding to modeled rest; reserve its interval so overlapping step energy is replaced, not added again.
  • Keep elapsed-so-far energy separate from a projected full day. Preserve fractional accumulation, split local-day boundaries and retain the profile/model revision used.
  • Resting expenditure may be modeled during trusted-clock downtime by an explicit policy; active and sleep observations remain missing. Never fill sensor gaps with fabricated measurements.
  • Mifflin–St Jeor and MET tables are population models. No precise personal calorie-accuracy claim follows from successful arithmetic or simulator screenshots.

A supported model can use mass, height, age and an explicitly selected coefficient. It estimates resting expenditure; it does not measure metabolism. See the original Mifflin–St Jeor paper and the Compendium explanation of MET limitations.

Acceptance gates

  • Profile: migrations of existing preferences, unknown fields, decimal weight, units, override/eligibility, effective-date changes, SD absent/full/readback-failure and dirty retry.
  • Accounting: hand-calculated rest/net-active/total vectors; workout/steps overlap; pause/reboot/idempotence; midnight, DST, timezone travel, forward/backward clock corrections and unknown time.
  • History/recovery: power failure at each commit phase, damaged tail/bank, card removal/swap, schema upgrade, bounded queues, correction replay, backup/restore and authorized export/delete.
  • Motion: real-device sampling/units/calibration first; labeled steps, sitting, off-wrist, work/sport motion, overnight and nap diaries; held-out traces. Simulator uses the same replay engine and labels synthetic inputs.
  • Power: measured screen-off/day/overnight/workout current or controlled discharge with diagnostic modes on/off; low-power transitions and recovery. Existing voltage ADC alone cannot attribute component current.
  • Integration: source-exact build and simulator/device smoke of all health apps, home/faces and required OS apps, plus SD-write/radio/recording coexistence and long-session soak.
  • Performance: preserve required 30 fps and blur policy. Prior exact-source baseline missed 87/861 intervals (max 45.504 ms); resolving that pre-existing gap is a separate uncertain release dependency, not a passed health check.
